Halvorsen Lab Equipment Checkout Policy (fictional)

1. Purpose
This policy decides whether a person may check out lab equipment.

2. Definitions
2.1 Every person has exactly one role: enrolled student, staff, or visitor.

3. Checkout rule
3.1 A person may check out lab equipment if and only if their role is enrolled student or staff.
3.2 Visitors cannot check out lab equipment, regardless of their safety training.
