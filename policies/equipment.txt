Halvorsen Lab Equipment Checkout Policy (fictional)

1. Purpose
This policy decides whether a person may check out lab equipment.

2. Definitions
2.1 Every person has exactly one role: enrolled student, staff, or visitor.
2.2 A person's safety training is either current or not current. Training that has expired, or that was never completed, is not current.

3. Checkout rule
3.1 A person may check out lab equipment if and only if their role is enrolled student or staff, and their safety training is current.
3.2 Visitors cannot check out lab equipment, regardless of their safety training.
3.3 A person whose safety training is not current cannot check out lab equipment, regardless of their role.
