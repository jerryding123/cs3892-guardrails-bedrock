Northfield Analytics Leave Eligibility Policy (fictional)

1. Purpose
This policy decides whether an employee is eligible for paid leave.

2. Definitions
2.1 An employee is either full-time or part-time.
2.2 Months of service is the number of whole calendar months the employee has completed since their hire date. Partial months do not count.

3. Eligibility rule
3.1 An employee is eligible for leave if and only if the employee is full-time and has completed at least 12 months of service.
3.2 A part-time employee is not eligible for leave, regardless of months of service.
3.3 An employee who has completed fewer than 12 months of service is not eligible for leave, regardless of full-time or part-time status.
